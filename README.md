# Andy

Andy is an AI agent for small businesses. It answers customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and your website, books appointments, sells from your catalog, and opens a ticket when a person needs to step in. You can take over any conversation at any time.

**Try it:** [andypartner.com](https://andypartner.com) · **Docs:** [docs.andypartner.com](https://docs.andypartner.com)

## Status

We're open-sourcing Andy. The code is moving here from our private repository over the next few weeks, after a full audit. Watch or star the repo to see it land.
